우리 연구실의 이시열 학생, 김선호 학생, 서민경 학생이 TUS-REC challenge에서 4위를 차지했습니다.

<img src='/images/news/2024/TUS_수상_시열쌤.png' style="width: 100%; max-width: 1000px; height: auto;" alt="Responsive Image">

We participated TUS-REC challenge to validate our current approaches, which is 3D US volume reconstruction from conventional 1D array transducer by Deep Learning-based scan motion estimation.
Our submission ranked 4 th, and we will expand our results to the 3D Photoacoustic imaging or Doppler imaging for the 3D vessel visualization.
